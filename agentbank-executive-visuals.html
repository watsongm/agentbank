01 · The Channel Collapses
Apps stop being where day-to-day banking happens.
▾
Standalone mobile apps persist for deep inspection and edge cases, but routine financial actions — sweeping surplus cash, paying bills, pre-qualifying for credit — move into conversation with the customer's primary agent.
Channel Shift: App Sessions → Autonomous MCP Tool Calls
02 · Products Become Tools
The unit of value shifts from screens to callable capabilities.
▾
If a banking product isn't exposed as a clean, schema-validated MCP / OpenAPI tool, an agent cannot discover or invoke it — and the customer never experiences it.
agentBANK Standard: 10 BIAN v12 Domains → 14 Typed Tools
03 · Consent Replaces Logins
Scoped permission becomes the core UX primitive.
▾
Time-bound OAuth 2.0 scopes (accounts:read payments:write), spending caps, and RFC 9449 DPoP sender-constrained tokens matter more than passwords and browser cookies.
Security Profile: FAPI 2.0 + DPoP Proof Replay Protection
04 · Audit Replaces Support Tickets
Disputes are resolved by replaying cryptographic reasoning trails.
▾
What the agent observed, planned, and executed in the SHA-256 hash-chained ledger is the single source of truth — for the customer, the bank's compliance team, and the regulator.
Compliance: Immutable SHA-256 Hash Chain + OTel Waterfalls
05 · Distribution Changes
Whoever agents prefer to call wins the customer relationship.
▾
Banks compete on schema clarity, sub-250ms deterministic execution, and transparent terms — not on app store rankings or advertising spend.
Competitive Moat: Deterministic APIs & Instant Settlement
Executive Takeaway
One agent, many rails. The consumer's relationship is with their agent; each provider's relationship is won through the reliability and clarity of the tool calls the agent chooses to make.
